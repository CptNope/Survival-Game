# Support

Stuck, found a bug, or the game will not load? Here is where to ask.

- **Email:** [info@jeremyanderson.tech](mailto:info@jeremyanderson.tech). Best for anything about a download, a donation, or a problem you would rather not post in public.
- **Bugs and ideas:** [open an issue](https://github.com/CptNope/Survival-Game/issues). Say which browser and device you are on and what you were doing when it went wrong.
- **Comments:** the [itch.io page](https://cptnope.itch.io/survival-game-web-demo) has a comment thread for quick questions.

The Wilds is made by [Jeremy Anderson Tech](https://jeremyanderson.tech/).
